Why Kinetix Fit

You don’t need a perfect week. You need a next step.

Fitness shouldn’t be all or nothing. Kinetix Fit is built for real weeks: the busy ones, the rainy ones, the ones where you only manage a walk. Steady progress is the point, and we reward it.

  • Progress over perfection

    Everyday goals sized for a normal day, not an athlete’s. Miss one? Tomorrow is a fresh start.

  • Real data, not guesswork

    Points come from what your phone or watch actually recorded, through Apple Health or Health Connect. So they mean something.

  • Rewards you can taste

    Swap your points for a donation to a UK charity, or a coffee on us. Proper rewards for steady effort.

  • Made in the UK, private by design

    Targets follow NHS guidance, the food list knows porridge from palak paneer, and your gut checks, cycle and body stats never leave your phone.

Features

Built around you.

Every number below is what the app works out for Maya, our example member: 29, 165 cm, 62 kg, and exercising three to five days a week.

01Nutrition

Targets built on your body.

Tell Kinetix Fit your height, weight, age and how active you are. It works out your calories, protein and fibre for the day, and your BMI suggests a goal to start from. Then every food you log counts towards 11 nutrients.

  • Calories
  • Protein
  • Carbs
  • Fat
  • Fibre
  • Saturated fat
  • Sugars
  • Salt
  • Potassium
  • Iron
  • Calcium
  • In the UK your targets follow NHS guidance, and each of the other eight countries uses its own.
  • Iron and calcium follow your age and sex: Maya needs 14.8 mg of iron a day because she’s under 51.
  • Salt, sugars and saturated fat are limits to stay under, not goals to hit.

02Meal ideas

Ideas for what’s left of your day.

By dinner, Maya still needs 44 g of protein. Kinetix Fit ranks over 100 everyday meals by what’s left of her targets, her goal and the time of day, and leaves out anything her diet or allergies rule out. Each idea is real foods in stated amounts, with USDA nutrition.

  • Free: three ideas at a time, each with the reason it fits.
  • Dishes people eat where you live, from dal with rice to salmon with sweet potato.
  • Plus asks our AI for nine more, built from your goal, BMI and everything you’ve eaten and done today.

03Food logging

Scan it, snap it or just type it.

Scan a barcode and the pack’s own label gives the nutrition and allergens. Take a photo of your plate and each food gets its own entry and amount. Or type “2 roti and dal” and it’s logged in a second, worked out on your phone.

  • Every food is checked against your allergies and diet before it’s added.
  • Nutrition from USDA and Open Food Facts, and the app always says which.
  • Typing a food is free and unlimited, with 2 photo or barcode scans a day (10 with Plus).

04Cycle

Your cycle, in plain words.

Log the day each period starts. Kinetix Fit learns your own rhythm instead of assuming 28 days, then shows your phase, your fertile window and when your next period is likely: a window of dates, and how sure it is.

  • Predictions from your last six cycles, and a note to see your GP if they vary a lot, as the NHS advises.
  • Short sleep, hard training or eating too little this cycle? It says so, because they can delay a period.
  • It stays on your phone, never sent to us or to our AI. An estimate to understand your cycle, not a way to prevent pregnancy.

05Workouts

No watch? No problem.

Your phone counts your steps on its own, and a two-tap check-in covers your sleep and energy. Went to yoga or played five-a-side? Add it in a few taps, from 24 activities or one you name yourself. Wear a watch and heart rate, sleep and VO₂ max join in.

  • Steps from your phone through Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Any length, today or any day in the past year, with a note if you like.
  • Workouts you add are your own record. Quests and points only count what your phone or watch records.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Email info@kinetixfit.co.uk and a real person will reply.

When can I get Kinetix Fit?

Kinetix Fit is being tested now and is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Join early access and we’ll email you the day it’s ready. Already have an account? You can log in on the web today.

Is Kinetix Fit free?

Yes. Food logging, check-ins, quests, points and charity donations are all free. Kinetix Fit Plus is an optional subscription that adds more photo and barcode scans (10 a day instead of 2), coffee vouchers, AI meal ideas, extra widgets and more app icons. You’ll see the price in the app before you decide.

How do rewards work?

You earn points for things your phone or watch can confirm, like steps and sleep, plus daily check-ins and food checks. At 1,000 points you can give £2.50 to a UK charity or, if you’re a Plus member, get a £5 coffee voucher, one a month. Rewards are UK-only for now.

How long does it take to earn a coffee?

Doing your daily goals every day earns about 1,000 points a month, so a coffee takes about a month. A lighter routine takes longer, and every point still counts.

Do I need a smartwatch?

No. Your phone counts steps on its own. A watch or fitness band adds heart rate, sleep and more. Anything that shares with Apple Health or Health Connect works, such as an Apple Watch or a Galaxy Watch.

How accurate is the food logging?

Nutrition comes from USDA FoodData Central and, for barcodes, Open Food Facts, and the app always shows where a number came from. Photo scans estimate portions, so you can change an amount in a tap. If you have a serious allergy, always check the packaging too.

What happens to my health data?

Kinetix Fit only reads health data you allow, and you can disconnect at any time. Gut checks, periods and body stats like blood pressure stay on your phone. We never sell your data. The details are in our privacy policy.

Does it work outside the UK?

Yes. The app supports nine countries, including Ireland, the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, India, Singapore and the UAE, with local foods, units and guidance. Rewards are UK-only for now.

Is Kinetix Fit medical advice?

No. Kinetix Fit is a wellness app, not a medical device. Targets and suggestions are general guidance, so talk to your GP before big changes, especially if you have a health condition or are pregnant.

Who is behind Kinetix Fit?

Kinetix Fit is made by JN Global Ventures LTD, a company registered in England and Wales and based in Sheffield. You need to be 16 or over to use it.
