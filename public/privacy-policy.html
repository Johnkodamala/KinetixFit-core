Kinetix Fit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Kinetix Fit is operated by JN Global Ventures LTD ("we", "us", "our"), a company registered in England and Wales, company number 17268312, registered office in Sheffield, United Kingdom. We are registered with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), registration reference ZC236047.

This policy explains what personal data Kinetix Fit collects, why, and how it's handled, in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to anyone who uses the Kinetix Fit app or website (kinetixfit.co.uk). Kinetix Fit is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

2. What data we collect

Account & profile data
Name, email address, height, weight, age, sex, fitness goal/target, and any allergens you tell us about.

Health & biometric data (special category data)
If you choose to connect a device, we access data from Apple Health, Google Health Connect, or Oura Ring — including heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, sleep data, step count, workouts, distance, calories burned, blood oxygen, breathing rate, blood pressure, VO2 max, weight and body fat. Blood oxygen, breathing rate, blood pressure, VO2 max, weight and body fat are only shown to you in the app and never leave your phone. This is only accessed with your explicit permission, granted through Apple's, Google's, or Oura's own consent screens, and only for as long as you keep the connection active. You can disconnect at any time in-app.

Food & nutrition data
Photos you take of meals (processed to identify food and estimate nutrition, not stored longer than necessary to return a result), barcode scans, and manually entered meals, along with the nutrition data calculated from them.

Rewards & activity data
Points earned, quests completed, redemption history, and donation pledges.

Payment & subscription data
We do not directly store your card details. Subscription payments and management are handled by RevenueCat and the Apple App Store / Google Play Store on our behalf.

Technical data
Standard technical data needed to operate the app (device type, app version, error logs) for troubleshooting.

Early access list
If you join the early access list on our website, we keep your email address and, if you tell us, which kind of phone you use. We use them only to email you about Kinetix Fit's launch. To stop the form being abused we also keep a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address, for up to an hour.

You can withdraw consent for health data processing at any time by disconnecting your device — this doesn't affect the rest of your account.

4. Who we share data with

We share data only with the specific service providers needed to run Kinetix Fit, each acting under their own data protection obligations:

  • RevenueCat — subscription and payment management
  • Vercel — app hosting
  • Upstash — backend data storage (points, redemption records)
  • Anthropic — processes meal photos to identify food (image only, not your full profile)
  • Open Food Facts / USDA FoodData Central — nutrition lookups (product/food data only, no personal data sent)
  • Tremendous — processes coffee voucher redemptions (your email only, when you redeem)
  • JustGiving — processes charity donations (once live)
  • Apple Health / Google Health Connect / Oura — only data you explicitly connect and authorise

We do not sell your personal data to anyone, for any purpose.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK/EEA (e.g. in the United States). Where this happens, we rely on their compliance with UK-approved data transfer mechanisms (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

5. How long we keep your data

We keep account and health data for as long as your account is active, plus a reasonable period afterward for legal/accounting purposes. You can request deletion at any time (see Section 7).

If you joined the early access list, we keep your email until you ask us to remove you or we no longer need it for the launch. You can ask to be removed at any time by emailing info@kinetixfit.co.uk or using the unsubscribe link in our emails.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted storage of sensitive data and access controls on our backend systems.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time (for health data specifically)
  • Complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk) if you're unhappy with how we've handled your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@kinetixfit.co.uk.

8. Data Protection Officer

Our appointed Data Protection Officer can be contacted at info@kinetixfit.co.uk.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Kinetix Fit's features evolve. We'll update the "Last updated" date above and notify you of significant changes in-app.
