Kinetix Fit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Kinetix Fit is operated by JN Global Ventures LTD ("we", "us", "our"), a company registered in England and Wales, company number 17268312, registered office in Sheffield, United Kingdom. We are the controller of your personal data.

This policy explains what personal data the Kinetix Fit app (Android and iPhone) and website collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it. It follows UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, and is written to meet the requirements of Google Play and the Apple App Store.

In short

  • We collect the health, food and activity information you give us or connect, to run the app and your account.
  • We never sell your data, and we never use it for advertising. The app has no ads and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Health and cycle data is used only to give you the features you see in the app. It is shared only with the service providers listed below, only as far as needed to run those features.
  • You can delete your account or just your data inside the app at any time (see Section 7).

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to anyone who uses the Kinetix Fit app or website (kinetixfit.co.uk). Kinetix Fit is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

2. What data we collect

Account & profile data
Name, email address, height, weight, age, sex, fitness goal, activity level, diet (for example vegetarian), any allergens you tell us about, and your country and preferences (such as reminder times and theme). Your email address and a password are held by our sign-in provider, Supabase; we never see your password.

Health & biometric data (special category data)
If you choose to connect Apple Health (iPhone) or Google Health Connect (Android), the app reads: steps, distance, heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, sleep, workouts (exercise), active and total calories burned, blood oxygen, breathing rate, blood pressure, VO2 max, weight and body fat. The app only reads this data; it never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect. Access is only with your explicit permission, through Apple's or Google's own consent screens, and you can change or withdraw it at any time in the Health app, in Health Connect or in Kinetix Fit.

Readings the app shows you are saved to your Kinetix Fit account (so they appear on your other devices and in your trends and weekly views). A short-lived copy of today's steps, heart rate, HRV and sleep is also kept on our server for up to 2 days so we can check that a quest was really completed.

Food & nutrition data
Meals you log (typed, from a barcode scan or from a photo), saved foods, and the nutrition calculated from them. Photos you take of meals are sent to our server to identify the food and are not stored by us after the answer comes back. Barcodes you scan are used only to look up the product.

Water, workouts and check-ins
Drinks you log, workouts you add by hand or that your phone records, and your daily morning check-ins (including how energetic you feel).

Gut checks and digestive wellbeing
Your answers to the daily gut check and the patterns the app finds in them. This is health data.

Period (cycle) data
If you use period tracking, the dates you log and the predictions the app works out from them. This is health data and is sensitive. It is used only to show you your own cycle in the app. It is not sent to Anthropic or any advertiser, and it is not used to infer anything about you outside the app.

Rewards & activity data
Points, XP, streaks, quests completed, voucher redemptions and charity donation records.

Payment & subscription data
We do not store your card details. Subscriptions are bought and managed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, and RevenueCat tells us whether your Plus subscription (or a promo code) is active. RevenueCat identifies you by your account email address.

Location
We do not collect your GPS or precise location. We use the country you choose (or your phone's language setting suggests) and your phone's time zone to show the right foods, rewards and days.

Technical data
Standard technical data needed to run the app, such as app version and error logs, and a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address on our website forms to stop abuse. The barcode scanner uses Google's ML Kit, which scans on your phone; Google may collect technical diagnostics such as crashes and performance from ML Kit, as described in Google's ML Kit disclosure.

Early access list
If you join the early access list on our website, we keep your email address and, if you tell us, which kind of phone you use. We use them only to email you about Kinetix Fit's launch. To stop the form being abused we also keep a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address, for up to an hour.

Notifications
Reminders (water, movement breaks, gut check, streak) are scheduled on your phone itself. We do not send push notifications from our servers, and no notification token is collected.

4. Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers needed to run Kinetix Fit. They act on our instructions, under their own data protection obligations, and may use the data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — stores your account and your synced data (profile, food, water, workouts, check-ins, gut checks, period data, health readings, points) and handles sign-in.
  • Vercel — hosts our website and the servers the app talks to.
  • Upstash — short-term storage on our servers (daily limits, today's health snapshot, reward records, the early access list).
  • RevenueCat — manages Plus subscriptions and promo codes, identifying you by your email address.
  • Anthropic — an AI provider. For meal photos it receives the photo (and any note you type), and nothing that identifies you. For AI meal ideas (Plus) it receives your goal, sex, age, height, weight, activity level, diet and allergies, your calorie, protein and fibre targets, what you have eaten today, your steps, workouts and sleep for today, and your country. It does not receive your name, email, period data or gut check answers.
  • Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central — nutrition lookups (a food name or barcode only; no personal data).
  • Tremendous — sends coffee vouchers (your account email address, only when you redeem one).
  • JustGiving — processes charity donations (once live).
  • Apple and Google — run Apple Health, Health Connect and the App Store / Google Play, which handle purchases, and which only share your health data with us when you allow it.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone, for any purpose. We do not use or share your health or cycle data for advertising, marketing, data brokering or credit or insurance decisions. We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or users' safety.

International transfers. Some of these providers process data outside the UK and the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on UK-approved safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or Standard Contractual Clauses, or an adequacy decision.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest with our providers, access controls and row-level security so each account can only read its own data, and keeping secret keys on our servers and never in the app. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

7. Deleting your data and account

You can delete your data yourself, in the app: Account → Your data & privacy → Delete account or data. You can choose to delete your data and keep your account, or delete your account and all your data. Deleting is immediate and cannot be undone. It removes your account, profile, logs, health readings, gut checks, period data, points and rewards history from your phone and from our servers, and your subscription record with RevenueCat.

If you can't get into the app, see how to delete your account, or email info@kinetixfit.co.uk from the address you signed up with. We reply within one month.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it in your store's subscription settings. If you redeemed a voucher, our rewards provider Tremendous keeps its own record of that order under its own policy.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR (and, if you live in the European Economic Area, the EU GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data (most of it you can edit in the app)
  • Delete your data (see Section 7)
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for health data and anything else we rely on consent for
  • Complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority if you're unhappy with how we've handled your data

To exercise any of these rights, email info@kinetixfit.co.uk. We reply within one month.

If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law. You have similar rights to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and not to be discriminated against for using them. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. We treat health information as sensitive personal information and use it only to provide the app. Email us to make a request.
